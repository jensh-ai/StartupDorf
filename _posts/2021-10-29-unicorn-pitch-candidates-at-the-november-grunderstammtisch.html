On November 3, it’s that time again. For the fifth time this year, the StartupDorf Unicorn Pitch will take place. To our great pleasure, the pitches will now again take place on site – Super7000. Best conditions for all founders who pitch and of course best conditions for the spectators who can get a complete picture of the startup live.

So don’t miss this date and leave your RSVP at Meetup.com/StartupDorf. Please note our Corona Hygiene rules and our 2G admission policy.

Procedure of the Unicorn Pitch

The friendliest pitch competition – A pitch lasts 5 minutes, followed by a 2-4 minute question/answer round. A pitch can be presented in German or English. 3 teams present one after the other and the audience decides at the end which startup can take home the Unicorn trophy. The winning team is also qualified to participate in the Unicorn Pitch Grand-Prix 2022.

 

*Bion Analytics and Revotion are member startups from the StartupDorf and use the club platform to maintain a detailed web profile, among other things.
