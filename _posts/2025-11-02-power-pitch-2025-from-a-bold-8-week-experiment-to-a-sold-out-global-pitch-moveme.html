JUNE – The First Power Pitch (70+ Attendees, Built in Just 8 Weeks)

With only eight weeks of preparation, StartupDorf and WHU – Otto Beisheim School of Management, together with Heuking, launched the first Power Pitch.

More than 70 attendees joined the inaugural edition –  a clear sign that the region was hungry for a fresh, high-speed, founder-focused format. Mandy Yuman Sun as a WHU alumna, returning to campus to moderate felt like a homecoming. As a StartupDorf member of the extended board and initiator of the event, the energy in the room was incredibly rewarding.

What makes Power Pitch different?

• 2-minute elevator pitches

• No slides

• No long intros

• Only vision, voice, and clarity

The energy was electric — fast, authentic, and founder-centric.

Audience-Decided Pitch Finale

The night concluded with a live pitch battle between Katcare and Vaetro, decided directly by the audience.

Winner: Vaestro — Their clarity and delivery impressed the entire room.

The first Power Pitch proved that the region was ready for something new — and it set the stage for rapid expansion.

OCTOBER — Power Pitch Levels Up (130 Tickets Sold Out, 5 Countries Represented)

By October, Power Pitch returned — stronger, bolder, and more global. This time, 130 tickets sold out well in advance.


A Truly Global Stage

Founders traveled from:

• Finland

• Turkey

• Taiwan

• Brazil

• Germany

This diversity reflects a core value of Power Pitch: openness to global entrepreneurs and the ambition to build an international, inclusive startup community for NRW. With the event organization and international founder engagement led by our event implementation partner Global Spotlight GmbH, we are proud that Power Pitch has become a platform where global founders feel seen, welcomed, and empowered to share their ideas.

Winners Advancing to the Unicorn Pitch Grand Final 2026 (In Spring – don’t miss)

• @EventDaily, pitched by Lukas Steffens

 

Both teams demonstrated exceptional clarity and vision.
