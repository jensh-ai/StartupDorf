Your way to the guest list for …

The Unicorn Pitch Grand Final 2023 – the exclusive closing event of the Unicorn Pitch Series 2022.

… is via your registration on Eventbrite (priolink.com/gf23).

We ask for your understanding for access to our guest list:

StartupDorf is an open place. However, some formats, such as the Unicorn Pitch Grand Final, are exclusively reserved for our members and a few multipliers. But we also want to give “new” founders and creative innovators from the “corporate world” an insight into what we do. To this end, we are offering a limited number of tickets at a reasonable price, which should be understood as an “anti-no-show” component. We look forward to seeing you.

Don’t be discouraged by a possible waiting list, there are always cancellations or contingents are returned by jury members or sponsors.

If you belong to the group described above, put yourself on our waiting list.

Event Details:

The Unicorn Pitch has become another highlight of the StartupVillage and offers with the Grand Final the exclusive conclusion in the search for the best startup out of five qualifiers.
In the final the following startups will compete for the title “StartupDorf Unicorn of the Year”: ZeltzuHause, Click N Safe, Your Easy AI, Animate Me and Kaiako.
In addition to the pitches, our guests can enjoy culinary delights from Ingo Köthschneider and his team. The event location Canonicus offers that certain something, so that our association members and multipliers will have fond memories of an exciting evening with lots of entertainment.

For the Pitch Competition we could again win a competent jury from our association members, who will decide at the end which startups will receive prize money and material prizes from the sponsors in addition to the fame and glory of the evening.

Again on the jury:
Dr. Paolo Bavaj , Chief Innovation and Development Officer, Stahl Holdings.

New to the jury as a startup representative:
Dr. Monika Hauck, Founder of Repair Rebels
New to the jury as a representative of supporting members:
Dr. Patrick Müller, Equity Partner at Heuking Kühn Lüer Wojtek
Christian Wenzel, Founder Venture Labs

To get on our guest list, you need to go through our waiting list on Eventbrite (accessible here: www.priolink.com/gf23). We will then see if we can add you to the list (capacity vs. cancellations).
