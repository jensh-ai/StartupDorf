Don’t miss. On December 7, the last Gründerstammtisch (Founders’ Roundtable) of the year will take place.

The motto of the last StartupDorf Stammtisch of the year is “Rock & Climb Your Business”! One thing is for sure, after this Stammtisch we can start the new year creatively, courageously and successfully!

Annika Lyndgrun will show us the mental and emotional impact of music and how we can use it as founders for more motivation and stamina.

Eugenia Gagin takes us into the world of climbing and shows us exciting parallels to the topic of leadership. You will not only learn why you should regularly learn traps, but also which scientific facts lie behind the topic of leadership and climbing.

You can expect two exciting lectures, cool drinks and of course a lot of networking!

More info on the talks are on the event page on Eventbrite.

Please note:

Registration required. Limited places.

The last Gründerstammtisch (Founders' Roundtable) of a year is always something special. The last stressful weeks before the vacation season and the turn of the year are especially challenging for founders and all startup people. With us you can gather energy for this purpose once again and also enjoy already realized successes. For StartupDorf, the year has already been super successful. Keywords here our 100th Stammtisch and the first Grand-Final of the Unicorn Pitch Series, and so much more ... So come by, we are looking forward. One day after Santa Claus the good old Saint Nicholas!!! Any mulled wine in da StartupDorf? Yes of course! 🎅🎄

We start at 7pm and look forward to welcoming many new and old friends of the Rhineland startup scene.
