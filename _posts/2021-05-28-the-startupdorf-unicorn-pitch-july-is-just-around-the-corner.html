The friendly startup pitch competition

StartupDorf Unicorn pitches continue this summer. Two dates: July and August. On July 7 and August 4, three startups will have the chance to pitch their innovative concepts online in front of our audience of startup enthusiasts.

Startups with digital innovations from all industries can apply Germany-wide, as well as from neighboring BENELUX countries.

The winner will receive the fabled Unicorn Trophy, a StartupDorf e.V. mentoring and qualify for the Unicorn Pitch Grand-Prix in February 2022.

Startups apply now!

Info & Application http://www.startupdorf.de/pitching/

**Application deadline for the July pitch date is 6/16 Applicants will receive an acceptance or rejection letter on 6/18 for the StartupDorf Unicorn Pitch July date (7/7).

 

“Because it is important to pitch well, founders should definitely rehearse this in front of an audience”

This thought was the origin of today’s StartupDorf Unicorn Pitch. Years ago, the pitch competition, which was held by founders for founders, was a part of the “Gründerstammtisch” (regulars’ table for founders), but has now grown into an important highlight of the StartupDorf on 6 dates a year. In addition to the training effect, the feedback, the contacts as well as more visibility that comes with winning the Unicorn Trophy, the StartupDorf mentoring and the qualification for the Unicorn Pitch Grand Prix next year are now also in the foreground since 2021.

You can find more info about the competition here: Everything you should know about the Unicorn Pitch.

Get the Trophy and qualify for the Grand Prix Final in 2022!
