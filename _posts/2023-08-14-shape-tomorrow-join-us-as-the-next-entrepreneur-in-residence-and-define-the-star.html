Shape the future of innovation! Grow with the vibrant StartupDorf Hub in Düsseldorf and the whole Rhine-Ruhr region!

Passionate about innovation and entrepreneurship? Ready to team up with the dynamic StartupDorf crew to craft the next wave of disruptive projects and initiatives? We’re on the hunt for a driven Entrepreneur in Residence who’s geared up to dream big and shape the startup future in North Rhine-Westphalia.

What’s in it for you?

  • Hands-on experience: Manage and roll out projects and programs, closely aligning with our board of directors.
  • Make an impact: Inject your innovative ideas and drive creativity to elevate our association’s game.
  • Prime networking: Forge deep connections in the Rhine-Ruhr startup scene. Meet your potential co-founders, investors, and key players!
  • Skill up: Dive deep into collaborative sessions and sparring with our board, with an option for mentoring to elevate your personal and professional trajectory.

What should you bring to the table?

  • A higher education degree, preferably a Master’s, MBA, or PhD in STEM disciplines.
  • Initial professional experiences with a proven track record in managing projects of all sizes.
  • A fervor for the startup ecosystem and a belief in the might of community-driven innovation.
  • At least 3 months (or better, 6 months) of burning enthusiasm for StartupDorf.

Bonus Points:

  • Digital savvy, comfortable with agile methodologies and modern tech tools.
  • A penchant for remote work and a soft spot for invigorating co-working spaces.
  • Aspirations not just to shine as a part of the association but to blaze a trail as a future founder!

Perks for you:

  • Thriving environment in one of the oldest and most esteemed startup communities in North Rhine-Westphalia.
  • Opportunities to network internationally and tap into the global startup ecosystem.
  • Compensation for expenses and travel.
  • A stepping stone to potentially joining our expanded board of directors and deepen your ties in our network.

Support us as an Entrepreneur in Residence and step into an exhilarating journey where you don’t just make invaluable contacts but actively shape tomorrow’s startup landscape!
