As mentioned in an earlier post, we just had our first meteor Düsseldorf meetup in Gewächshaus Düsseldorf. It was a very informative and fun event as the pictures show. We were 20-25 people with a diverse background. Some were full-time Meteor developers, others had just heard about this amazing framework. I think most people grasped the convenience and power of Meteor to get up something very quickly. Besides, we were happy to offer some great deals for developers who wanted to start learning Meteor. You can still check them out on our  meetup page.
The next meetup will be in November (day tba). If you don’t want to miss the next meetup, join our Meetup Group or Facebook Group.
Hope to see you around for Meteor Düsseldorf #2!
