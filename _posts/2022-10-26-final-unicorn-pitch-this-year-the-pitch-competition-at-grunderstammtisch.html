Don’t miss it. On November 2, the last Unicorn Pitch of this year will start. The winning startup will automatically qualify for the StartupDorf Unicorn Pitch Grand Final, which will take place in spring 2023. Register now via Eventbrite, as the number of space is limited.

Special feature this time: the winners of the last pitches will also be in the audience. So even more founders for dialogue and networking.

Registration required. Limited places.

To win the qualifying round, a startup needs the most votes submitted, which will be determined in a secret audience poll. In the audience poll, our guests decide which ideas are best presented and promise the most success in terms of content.

You want to be there as a guest, do your voting and get to know the startup scene, innovation enthusiasts and other startup founders at the networking event later on! Then simply register and get your free ticket.

This time in the pitch: toshi-app, kaiako and granny-angels

Are you interested in which startups participated last time? Then please read our Unicorn Pitch Review.

Want to learn more about our startup pitch format the Unicorn Pitch? You can find it in the blogpost “Everything you should know about the Unicorn Pitch“. Are you interested in pitching your startup next year? In the next weeks we will communicate the dates for the new series. Then we will also update our application form.

A membership in the association is not necessary for an application to the pitch. Note: Our association would like to be represented by at least one member in the pitch.

 

We start at 7 pm and look forward to welcoming many new and old friends of the Rhineland startup scene.

Unicorn Pitch No. 17 in the course of the 108th StartupDorf Gründerstammtisch on Wednesday, November 2, 2022 in Düsseldorf.
To the Eventbrite registration.
Host of the evening: Hanns Tappen, Chairman StartupDorf e.V.
Location: Wacom Experience Center
