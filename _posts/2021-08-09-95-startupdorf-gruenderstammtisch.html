Dear Startup Friends!

Last week we had an unintentional premiere. For the first time in 8 years, we had to cancel the planned Founder’s Roundtable, on Aug. 4, at very short notice and reschedule it for a new date, Aug. 11.

Although the speakers and pitch candidates were all present, we could not get the guests into the room. Technical problem. We say sorry to all who were standing in front of a “closed” door and thus irritated had to plan their evening differently.

The good news: We will make up for the Stammtisch remote (zoom) on August 11 and are “Super Happy” 🤗 that all speakers and candidates were able to free up time for the repeat.

Super Commitment. Big thank you!

All that’s left to say is please join us on Wednesday, Aug. 11 from 6:55 p.m. to hear exciting keynotes and watch three startups pitch for the Unicorn Trophy.

Add Stammtisch to your Calendar including zoom meeting link

95. StartupDorf Gründerstammtisch – Startup Pitches and Lectures about Impact Startups, Fashion Industry, Sustainablitiy and Innovation

We are looking forward to Dr. Monika Hauck’s and Daniel Bartel’s keynotes on these topics.

Dr. Monika Hauck will talk about “Innovation and Sustainability in the Fashion Industry”. She is the founder of REPAIR REBELS & CHANGE ROOM and lectures and researches on sustainable innovation at WHU.
Monika is a member of StartupDorf e.V. with her startup “Repair Rebels”.

Daniel Bartel will talk about “Impact Startups & Transformative Entrepreneurship: How New Entrepreneurship is part of saving the world!” Daniel holds many roles. Among them, he is an innovator at MAK3it and a regional speaker at NRWalley. NRWalley is a network partner in StartupDorf and vice versa.

– All Keynotes and Pitches will be in English –
