With your vote you have at least 2% of the total voting result in your hand, maybe even more if we have not reached the maximum possible number of visitors of 50 people.

You realize that your vote counts for a lot. That’s why we thought that sometimes it’s good to have evaluation criteria at hand to make voting for the best pitch easier today.

Imagine you would give 1-3 points to each bullet point, then you could give a maximum of 18 points. But as we know that the gut feeling is very important, you should be able to follow it independent of the numbers 😉

Have fun Juror!

•Innovative nature of the business idea
•Addressed market, industry, competitive situation
•Customer benefit and demand
•Feasibility and perspective of the startup idea
•Founder personality/founder team
•Pitch quality
and gut feeling “Bauchgefühl”
