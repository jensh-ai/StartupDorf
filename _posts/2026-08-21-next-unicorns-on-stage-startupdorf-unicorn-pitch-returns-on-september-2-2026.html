Six startups. Five minutes to pitch. Two winners. And one evening bringing Düsseldorf’s startup ecosystem together.

On September 2, 2026, the StartupDorf Unicorn Pitch Series returns for its second qualifying round of the year. Once again, founders, investors, corporates and startup enthusiasts will meet at Mindspace Düsseldorf to discover new ventures, exchange ideas and connect with the community.

The Unicorn Pitch has become one of StartupDorf’s signature formats and an established meeting point for the startup ecosystem in Düsseldorf and beyond. The idea remains deliberately simple: put ambitious founders on stage, give them a limited amount of time and let both experts and the community decide which startups stand out.

Six startups. 5 + 5 minutes. Full focus.

Six startup teams will take the stage.

Each team gets:

  • 5 minutes to pitch
  • 5 minutes for questions and answers

Pitches can be delivered in either German or English.

And there is a special feature again this year: both the jury and the audience get a vote. At the end of the evening, two startups will leave the stage as winners of the September round – taking another step towards the Unicorn Pitch Grand Final.

The participating startups and jury members will be introduced ahead of the event through StartupDorf’s LinkedIn and Instagram channels.

https://www.linkedin.com/company/startupdorf/ – https://www.instagram.com/startupdorf

More than a pitch competition

Of course, the stage matters. But the Unicorn Pitch has always been about more than pitches and trophies.

The evening brings together founders at different stages, investors, corporate representatives, ecosystem builders and people who simply want to discover what is being built next. The relaxed networking around the competition is an essential part of the format – and one of the reasons the Unicorn Pitch has developed into a recurring meeting point for Düsseldorf’s founder community.

Whether you are looking for your next investment, a potential cooperation partner, entrepreneurial inspiration or simply want to meet people shaping the regional startup ecosystem: this is your evening.
