StartupDorf – A Voice for Startups: Why Your Experience and Opinion on Coworking Spaces Matters

The startup scene in Düsseldorf and its surroundings is growing, and we at StartupDorf e.V. are proud to have been an active part of this ecosystem from the very beginning, 10 years ago. Since our inception, we’ve been dedicated to supporting founders like you by providing platforms and resources that contribute to success. However, there’s still much to be done, especially when it comes to spaces for startups.

“One thing’s for sure: For many startups, alongside the right mindset, the right coworking space is an indispensable part of their founding story.” Hanns Tappen

We’re fortunate to have some of our supporting members directly involved in the coworking and office space industry, like Startplatz, Factory Campus, or Mindspace. These spaces have made it easy for us to recommend fantastic working environments. But diversity and a wealth of perspectives in the startup scene are crucial. That’s why we aim to offer a broad range of recommendations to avoid appearing biased.

A Strong Team for a Vital Mission

For this initiative, we’ve teamed up with betawerke, a supporting member of our association and experts in startup community architecture & development, corporate-startup engagement, and venture building. It’s not just their deep knowledge of community-building and development that sets betawerke apart but also their tireless support for the startup ecosystem in Düsseldorf & beyond. As a token of their commitment and support for our mission, betawerke has agreed to collaborate pro-bono on this project. This joint effort aims to provide you and other founders in the region with the best possible information and recommendations.

Your Experience & Opinion is Worth Its Weight in Gold!

You know best what a coworking space needs to offer to meet a startup’s requirements. By sharing your feedback in our survey, you’re not just helping us but also other founders to find the perfect workplace.

It only takes a few minutes, and your feedback can make a significant difference. You’ll be contributing to shaping the coworking offers in our region even better and more target-oriented. Click here to participate in the survey. Be an active part of our community and actively shape the startup scene in Düsseldorf and its surroundings!
