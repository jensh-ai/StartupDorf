Last Unicorn Pitch of 2025 – Six Startups, Two Winners and Pizza

On Wednesday, November 5, 2025, StartupDorf e.V. once again invited founders, investors and startup enthusiasts to Mindspace Düsseldorf for the next milestone of its Unicorn Pitch series – six startups, five minutes on stage, and one goal: the grand finale.

Six Startups, Six Visions
The stage belonged to six innovative teams, each with just five minutes to convince both the jury and the audience of their ideas:

 

  • Akteo with Gianluca Salierno
  • chunkx with Theresa Voß
  • OJIRA Soft with Michael Onwuatuegwu
  • Kregtech with Chiachun Hsieh
  • Space Era Germany with Kaif Ali
  • FLEXXI with Lejla Hadzimuratovic
A diverse mix of industries, languages, and perspectives: three pitches in German, three in English – with founders traveling from Berlin, Munich, Cologne, Düsseldorf, and beyond. This diversity once again highlights how vibrant and international the startup scene in North Rhine-Westphalia (NRW) has become. ♥️

The Jury: Experience Meets Innovation

The jury combined entrepreneurial experience with a sharp eye for innovation:

Susanne Kremeier – Future designer, supporting startups in their transition from idea to scalable business model

Thomas E. Hollwedel – Business Angel and digital advisor with over 25 years of experience in innovation and IoT

Eugenia Gagin and Michail Kosak from the StartupDorf team, who also moderated the evening

“Congratulations to the well-deserved winners! It was an honor for me to have played a role as a member of the jury.”- Susanne Kremeier

Together, the panel evaluated not only creativity and innovation, but also the ability to turn ideas into sustainable businesses – just as the Unicorn Pitch format intends.

The Winners: FLEXXI and chunkx Advance to the Grande Finale

After exciting pitches and a close decision, two startups stood out:

Audience Winner: FLEXXI Care (Lejla Hadzimuratovic)
Jury Winner: chunkx (Theresa Voß)

Both impressed in their own way: FLEXXI with a practical and impactful approach to elderly care, and chunkx with an innovative learning technology solution. Congratulations to both teams on their well-deserved entry into the Unicorn Pitch Finale 2025/26.

“An evening full of diverse pitches. It was a pleasure to support StartupDorf e.V. as a jury member together with Susanne Kremeier. I was delighted to present the audience award to flexxicare Personaldienstleistung GmbH – a team that was also high on my personal list” – Thomas E. Hollwedel

 

An Evening Full of Energy, Ideas and Networking
The event offered not only strong pitches but also plenty of opportunities for networking and exchange. Founders, investors, and startup enthusiasts came together to connect, share insights, and celebrate entrepreneurship.

The atmosphere at Mindspace Düsseldorf was characterized by curiosity, courage, and genuine founder spirit – exactly what defines the StartupDorf community.

Thanks to All Supporters and Partners ♥️
A big thank-you goes to our partners betawerke and Mindspace, who helped make the evening possible – and to all the founders who brought passion, creativity, and commitment to the stage.
