Meet new StartupDorf club members on May 5. The startups Bounce Experts and Beautinda as well as our new supporting member OVHCloud will each introduce themselves in 15 minutes. Register here.

From East to West could be the subtitle. If you classify our member startups Beautinda from Essen and Bounce Experts from Geldern geographically, it shows very nicely our growing catchment area in NRW. In this case, we’re talking about the beautiful Ruhr area in the east and the just as charming left Lower Rhine.
And since startups in the digital economy have to do with technology and need strong partners to develop it, we are happy to introduce our newest supporting member in the field of digital tech on Wednesday: OVHCloud with its Startup Program.

Agenda for the Gründerstammtisch* No. 92

From East to West – Introducing of new club members.

Program / Agenda / Speakers

I. Welcome to the StartupDorf:

StartupDorf in a Nutshell (15 min)
What, Why and Who – why it makes sense to become a member of one of the oldest startup associations in Germany.
News: Partner & Projects @ StartupDorf

Host of the evening: Dr. Fabian Braun and Hanns Tappen (both members of the board)

II. Get to know new Startups and Supporting Members of StartupDorf:

Torsten Zühlsdorff, Bounce Experts (Startup Member).
Bounce Experts develops plugin technology for store operators to operate a professional bounce management in e-commerce and thus reduce the often very high bounce rates. (Bounce Experts is located in Geldern, district of Kleve. To the web profile)

Can Kaplan, Beautinda (Startup Member)
Beautinda mediates via appropriate APP technology cheap and flexible talented beauty artists from the surrounding area, who are not bound to regular opening hours. ( Beautinda is based in Essen, Germany. To the web profile)

Ray Farid, OVHcloud Startup Program (Supporting Member**).
Since 2015, over 1,800 startups and scaleups from around the world have participated in the OVHcloud Startup Program and benefit from this growing ecosystem.

III. Networking (the virtual/online edition):

We have found a virtual space thanks to our event hall on MixUp.world that allows us to approximate real networking with our regular attendees again. We are happy to welcome again many new and old Stammtisch people from the startup world on the Rhine and Ruhr and beyond (last time startup colleagues from Zurich (Switzerland).

Fun Fact about the current startup Gründerstammtisch “from East to West”: Did you know that our association’s territory (club region) is comparable to Silicon Valley in terms of spatial expansion?

You already realize, our ambitions for our wonderful region are ambitious. The StartupDorf is committed to first class entrepreneurship on the Rhine and Ruhr. Our vision is that our region (Metropolregion Rhein Ruhr) evolves from the center of Düsseldorf to a first tier startup hub in Europe.
