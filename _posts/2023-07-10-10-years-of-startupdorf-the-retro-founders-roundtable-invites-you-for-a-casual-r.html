Hey StartupDorf Family!

Ten fantastic years have passed, and we’re going back to our roots. Do you remember our very first public meetings on the sidewalk in front of Seifen Horst? How the roundtable has grown over time, from small and exquisite gatherings to 115 smashing events that have shaped the founder and startup scene in Düsseldorf and its surroundings?

Now we invite you to come and celebrate with us, to revive old times – at the Retro Roundtable! Our 10-year anniversary is around the corner and we’re celebrating it with a traditional roundtable. No agenda, no program – just good conversations, networking, and maybe a reunion with old acquaintances from the early years.

Are you curious about who will join from the old faces? We are too! Our Retro Roundtable is the high-school reunion of the Düsseldorf founder and startup scene! Whether you’ve only been there once or you’re a roundtable veteran, we look forward to seeing you!

And if you’re joining us for the first time, no problem! Our roundtable is, as always, open to makers and enthusiasts from the founder and startup scene. Join us on August 2nd at 7 pm on the sidewalk in front of the Seifen Horst bar at Neusserstraße 125 in Düsseldorf. There might even be surprises!

But don’t forget: everyone pays for their beer – their drinks themselves, just like in the good old days 😉.

Now, there’s only one thing left for you to do: Sign up for our event on Eventbrite at https://www.eventbrite.com/e/670639028387. It’s free! And then? Then tell everyone who should know about our event. Use the hashtags #10JahreStartupDorf, #StartupDorf, and #Gründerstammtisch and let’s ride the retro wave together!

See you on August 2nd! Until then, stay curious and engaged. Here’s to the next ten years of StartupDorf!

As Hanns Tappen, our initiator and founding chairman, likes to say:

It was those first roundtable meetings that had a special charm – simply authentic and full of passion. That’s what StartupDorf is all about. We look forward to a reunion at the Retro Roundtable!
