Meteor.js is finally crushing Düsseldorf. Meteor.js is an open-source platform for building top-quality web apps in a fraction of the time using JavaScript all the way and leveraging the power of full stack reactivity. If you are interested in developing web applications and are in or around Düsseldorf you should definitely join us. “Meteor Düsseldorf” is part of special-theme meetups powered by StartupDorf.
We are going to kick-off our brand new community with talks from two renowned experts and authors in the Meteor.js hemisphere. Manuel Schoebel and Stephan Hochhaus are going to explain Meteor.js and its benefits to you.
So, from all frameworks out there, why on earth Meteor (no pun intended ;))? Because it’s perfect to build MVPs effectively and efficiently.  It’s even considered the perfect match for lean start-ups and the killer of ruby on rails, respectively.
Interested? It doesn’t matter whether you are a beginner or an expert in Meteor.js. Share your knowledge, apps, and get feedback from others. Join the Meteor Düsseldorf community now and come to our first meetup ever in Düsseldorf on Tuesday, September, 16th.
We are very grateful to the people behind gewächshaus düsseldorf for hosting the Meteor Düsseldorf Meetup. Gewächshaus düsseldorf is a beautiful co-working space close to Düsseldorf’s central station. You should definitely check it out and hopefully more start-up events are going to be held there.
Alright! For more info, don’t forget to visit the Meteor Düsseldorf page on Meetup or the Meteor Facebook group.
