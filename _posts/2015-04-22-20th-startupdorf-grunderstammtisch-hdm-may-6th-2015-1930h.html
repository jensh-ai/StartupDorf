It is time to Meetup again!

On 6 May we get together for the next StartupDorf Stammtisch. The traditional startup people meetup for the digital economy in Düsseldorf.

Session No. 20 (our first little jubilee 😉 ) will give us again inspiring talks, presentations and of course valuable networking and intensive exchange opportunities.

Please register for the meetup using the link above.

Who are the presenters? 

Valentin Schütt, Seven Miles Group (Seven Miles GmbH is one of our Supporting Member of StartupDorf) and Marcus Hillebrecht, MINTANO (MINTANO is one of our StartupDorf member startups)

More event details is available on our Meetup.com event page.  

Don’t not forget to RSVP. Limited space/capacity. 

You never visited us before?

Founders, Startup People, Corporate Expats and international entrepreneurs are very welcome. Conversation in English and German.

  • open-house from 1930, watch for ‘startup people’ near to the entrance area in the lobby bar or in the conference room, where we start on 2000 with a 1,5h-program
    • Marcus Hillebrecht, MINTANO (30min)
    • Valentin Schütt, Seven Miles Group (30min)
    • Moderation, Open-MIC Session, 5-min-pitch-slots (30min)
    • —- End of the regular program —-
    • Networking approx. 1.5h (bar and hotel lounge)
  • casual, easy conversation about startup life, entrepreneurship, new ventures, …
  • a simple startup drinkabout, with some free beer for startup people
  • online registration needed
  • NO StartupDorf e.V. membership is needed
  • our typical guest: founder, startup people, devs and freelancer from DUS and region Düsseldorf
  • tweets or fb comments highly appreciate
