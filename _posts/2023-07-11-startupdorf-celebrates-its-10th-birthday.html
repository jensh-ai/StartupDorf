Sometimes it’s the small seeds that end up growing the biggest trees. That’s how it was with StartupDorf, which was originally launched as a community for founders in 2013. Today, 10 years later, we can proudly look back on an impressive track record and a vibrant and growing community. Each one of our 150+ events, 220+ pitches, and 8 different event formats were milestones on our joint journey. And now the next one is coming up: Our 10th anniversary!

On August 9, we celebrate this special milestone in the impressive setting of the NRW-Forum Museum Gastronomy Pong at the Ehrenhof in Düsseldorf. Of course, we want to share this day with as many members of our community as possible, but new faces are also very welcome.

But please note: The contingent of tickets for non-members is limited. We are offering a certain number of tickets at a discounted admission price. So if you’ve always wanted to be part of our community or have attended our public events, don’t hesitate to contact us so we can send you the password and code for our ticket page to sign up: https://www.eventbrite.com/e/672947031687

We are looking forward to all the familiar and new faces who want to celebrate this special day with us.
