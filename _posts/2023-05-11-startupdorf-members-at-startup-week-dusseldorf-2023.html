As in previous years, StartupDorf is not represented at the Startup Woche Düsseldorf itself, as we do not want to compete with our association / club members. Exceptions, of course, prove the rule: exceptions were the DUS Highflyer, which we organised for Düsseldorf Airport and the City of Düsseldorf, and the event “Corporate Innovation and Corporate Venture Capital – from Silicon Valley to Rhine Valley”, with which we brought US Ambassador John B. Emerson from Berlin to the first Startup Woche 2016 as part of an event cooperation with the US Consulate and WHU. Highlights that are still shining today. With our Startup Community Developer Hackathon “Dorfhack“ in 2019, we have underlined our role as a community builder with a strong international focus.
This year’s Startup Week will once again feature an exciting programme. This is also thanks to our association members, who bring Startup Week to life with their events here at the end of May.
Please check out the curated list (Google spreadsheet) of our members’ events for Startup Woche 2023 and secure your ticket directly.
Further down the list you will find a preview of the StartupDorf events in June, which will include 10 events and, for the first time in a long time, the Dorfschule. Some of our members will be providing great content for founders and people interested in starting a business with their workshops, seminars and talks. Tickets can be booked now.
