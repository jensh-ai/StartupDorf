2 minutes. No slides. Full attention.

On June 11, 2025, we’re launching Ninja Pitch & Networking – a fast-paced new event format powered by StartupDorf and the WHU – Otto Beisheim School of Management, Campus Düsseldorf.

Why a Pitch Format?

Pitch events are more than competitions – they are performance labs for founders.
They push you to communicate your idea clearly, convincingly, and with impact. Or as Karin Klein, founding partner at Bloomberg Beta, puts it:

“Simplicity is helpful and shows a clarity of vision and the ability to sell.”

The Ninja Pitch is a perfect addition to our well-known Unicorn Pitch Series.

While the Unicorn Pitch gives startups a stage to explain their model with visuals, slides, and Q&A, the Ninja Pitch challenges them to get straight to the point – with nothing but their voice and idea. It’s about storytelling, clarity, and message discipline. (- Hanns Tappen)

What is the Ninja Pitch?

At the Ninja Pitch, 4–6 selected startups get exactly 2 minutes to present their idea – verbally only, no slides, no deck.
Just your story. Pure and powerful.

A jury composed of a WHU representative and a StartupDorf board member selects the winning team, which earns:

  • An extended Spotlight Pitch at the end of the evening

  • A dedicated feedback session from the jury

  • A Wildcard spot at the second Ninja Pitch in October 2025

  • The chance to qualify for the Unicorn Pitch Grand Final 2025

 

Why this format matters

Clarity over show. Focus over flash.
If you can convince in 2 minutes, you’ve truly understood your core proposition.

The Ninja Pitch puts founders to the test – in the best sense.
It’s a platform for authentic, direct startup communication that brings together international talent, startup energy, and an honest community vibe.

What to expect

  • 4–6 bold startup pitches in 2 minutes each

  • Jury decision & Spotlight Pitch

  • Open networking with founders, investors, and ecosystem enablers

  • A welcoming, diverse community spirit

  • International ideas with local impact
